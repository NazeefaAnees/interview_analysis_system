
### **Software Developer**

**About Sitecore:**  
Sitecore is a leading digital experience platform trusted by global brands like American Express, Porsche, Starbucks, L’Oréal, and Volvo Cars. Our platform integrates content, commerce, and data to deliver personalized customer experiences.

**Responsibilities:**  
- Design, develop, and deliver high-quality features and bug fixes.
- Write clean, maintainable code aligned with Sitecore guidelines and best practices.
- Collaborate closely with Technical Managers, Leads, QA, and stakeholders.

**Qualifications:**  
- 3+ years of software development experience.

**Required Skills:**  
- Proficiency in C#/.NET, Typescript, and Terraform.
- Experience with Git and branching strategies.
- Database experience: SQL Server and NoSQL.
- Familiarity with DevOps practices, CI/CD pipelines, and testing (unit/integration).
- Experience in containerized application development (Docker, Kubernetes).

**Preferred Skills:**  
- Performance optimization and profiling experience.
- Familiarity with Visual Studio, VSCode, and ReSharper.
- Knowledge of modern SaaS architectures and Azure cloud services.

**Soft Skills:**  
- Excellent communication skills in English.
- Collaborative and supportive team-player.
- Passionate about continuous learning and innovation.
- Self-motivated and detail-oriented, comfortable under pressure.
- Eager to stay updated with technology trends.
