
### **Software Engineer – Java**

**Company:** Pearson  
**Location:** Colombo, Sri Lanka

**Role Overview:**  
As a Software Engineer at Pearson, you’ll play a key role in software engineering tasks, developing high-quality, maintainable code, and working collaboratively within dynamic, goal-driven project teams.

**Key Responsibilities:**  
- Design, develop, and deliver high-quality software solutions in Java (Spring Boot, Spring WebFlux - Reactive).
- Follow and advocate software engineering best practices and Pearson's Agile methodologies.
- Provide accurate estimates and ensure timely delivery of tasks.
- Conduct code reviews, mentor junior engineers, and maintain code quality standards.
- Address production incidents swiftly, providing timely fixes and improvements.
- Maintain strong collaboration and communication with global teams and stakeholders.

**Qualifications:**  
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, IT, Engineering, or related field.
- Minimum of 3 years experience in software engineering, web application development, and design.
- Solid experience with Java, Spring Boot, Spring WebFlux (Reactive), and JUnit.
- Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines, Bitbucket/GitLab, Agile practices, and IDEs.
- Strong understanding of Object-Oriented Programming and Design.
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, proficient in English.

**Preferred (additional) skills:**  
- Experience with Python 3.x.
- Familiarity with AWS services (Lambda, ECS, CloudFormation, CloudWatch, API Gateway).
