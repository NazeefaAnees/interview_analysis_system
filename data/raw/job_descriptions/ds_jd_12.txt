

### **Senior Machine Learning Engineer**

Join **OCTAVE** as a Senior Machine Learning Engineer, and play a critical role in developing, deploying, and optimizing advanced ML models and algorithms. You'll collaborate closely with data scientists, engineers, domain experts, and clients to address complex business challenges.

---

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Develop and deploy scalable machine learning models and pipelines.
- Implement state-of-the-art MLOps practices (feature stores, model monitoring, CI/CD, etc.).
- Optimize ML models for performance and reliability in production environments.
- Research and apply cutting-edge AI/ML techniques and tools.
- Collaborate cross-functionally to align technical solutions with business needs.
- Continuously monitor model performance and implement improvements.
- Maintain comprehensive documentation of code, models, and processes.
- Provide meaningful code reviews and effectively communicate insights and ideas.

---

**Technical Skills:**
- Bachelor's/Master's degree in Computer Science, AI, ML, or related fields.
- At least 1 year of experience developing and deploying ML models.
- Hands-on experience with PySpark optimization in cluster environments.
- Proficient in Python and ML frameworks (TensorFlow, PyTorch, scikit-learn, Keras).
- Experience deploying models using containerization (Docker/Kubernetes) and cloud platforms.
- Passionate about staying current with ML advancements and best practices.

**Soft Skills & Leadership:**
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, and collaborative abilities.
- Excellent communication and stakeholder engagement skills.
- Proven ability to manage projects independently, ensuring timely, high-quality delivery.
- Capable of mentoring junior team members.
