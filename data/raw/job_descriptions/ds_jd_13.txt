

### **Machine Learning Engineer**

**About Bilby:**  
Bilby is a Hong Kong-based technology firm specializing in analyzing global government policies using publicly available data. We quantify risks and opportunities, providing crucial insights into financial markets and business operations.

We are seeking a skilled Machine Learning Engineer to help us enhance our search capabilities, leverage AI models, and develop innovative data-driven solutions.

---

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Develop and optimize Elasticsearch-based search systems, writing complex queries for performance and accuracy.
- Integrate Large Language Models (LLMs), particularly using OpenAI APIs, to generate intelligent summaries and insights.
- Collaborate with full-stack engineers to deliver comprehensive data-driven products.
- Conduct ML research for document clustering, summarization, and practical graph database applications.

---

**Qualifications:**
- Proven experience with Elasticsearch and complex query optimization.
- Strong knowledge of AI models, especially LLMs and OpenAI integration.
- Experience with ML-driven clustering, summarization, and graph database searches.
- Familiarity with BigQuery and Neo4j or ability to quickly learn these technologies.
- Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and collaborative skills.

---

**Personal Attributes:**
- Thoughtful communicator with excellent interpersonal skills.
- Adaptive, practical, and detail-oriented.
- Results-driven and capable of rapid learning and application of new technologies.
