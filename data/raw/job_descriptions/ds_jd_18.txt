

### **AI Engineer**

**Key Requirements:**
- 1–2 years of hands-on experience in AI, including exploratory data analysis, data pipeline development, model building, and evaluation.
- Strong theoretical and practical knowledge of Machine Learning.
- Experience developing deep learning models, especially for NLP applications.
- Familiarity with Generative AI, advanced prompting techniques, and model fine-tuning.
- Experience building pipelines integrating externally hosted (and ideally locally hosted) Large Language Models (LLMs).
- Comprehensive experience implementing and evaluating Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines.
- Proficiency with orchestration frameworks (e.g., LangChain) for efficient LLM usage.
- Familiarity with evaluation frameworks for Generative AI text-generation (e.g., RAGAs, TruLens).
