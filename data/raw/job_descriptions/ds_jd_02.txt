
### **Data Scientist**

**Job Purpose:**  
We're seeking a skilled Data Scientist passionate about using data to drive strategic decisions. You'll develop analytics use cases, provide actionable insights, and support digital transformation by acquiring, modeling, and analyzing data.

**Key Responsibilities:**  
- Develop and implement data-driven solutions aligned with organizational goals.
- Apply advanced analytics techniques to generate actionable insights.
- Acquire, transform, and model data to inform strategic decisions.
- Collaborate cross-functionally to support digitization objectives.

**Qualifications:**  
- Degree in Engineering, Computer Science, Statistics, or IT-related field.
- Minimum 70% score on Data Scientist entry exam (external hires).

**Skills & Experience:**  
- Strong understanding of machine learning methods and algorithms.
- Proficiency in Python or R.
- Skilled in data visualization and clearly communicating findings.
- Familiarity with data engineering (data warehouses, big data, cloud).
- Eager to continuously update and enhance skills.
