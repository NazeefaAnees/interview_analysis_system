

### **Lead AI Engineer**

Join **Synopsys’ Generative AI Center of Excellence**, a team dedicated to advancing generative AI solutions across the company. As Lead AI Engineer, you'll design, develop, and deploy innovative AI/ML solutions, provide technical leadership, and collaborate with stakeholders to deliver cutting-edge products.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Design, develop, and deploy advanced AI and ML models to solve complex business challenges.
- Provide technical leadership and mentorship to junior engineers and data scientists.
- Collaborate cross-functionally with product managers and engineers to deliver AI solutions.
- Perform data analysis, preprocessing, and feature engineering for model development.
- Train, evaluate, fine-tune, and deploy machine learning models into production.
- Monitor model performance, ensuring accuracy and optimal operations.
- Document methodologies, models, and processes clearly for knowledge sharing.
- Ensure AI solutions comply with ethical standards and data privacy regulations.
- Stay current on AI advancements, applying them to enhance existing or new systems.

**Qualifications:**
- Bachelor's/Master’s degree (PhD preferred) in Computer Science, Data Science, Electrical Engineering, or related fields.
- Minimum 4 years of experience deploying AI/ML solutions in production environments.
- Strong proficiency in Python or C++ programming.
- Expertise with ML frameworks (TensorFlow, PyTorch, scikit-learn).
- Knowledge of statistical analysis, data mining, and visualization techniques.
- Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP) and container technologies (Docker, Kubernetes).
- Familiarity with Git and Agile methodologies.

**Skills:**
- Excellent problem-solving, analytical, and leadership abilities.
- Strong interpersonal and communication skills.
- Proven capability in mentorship and team collaboration.
