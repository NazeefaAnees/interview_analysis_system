- Strong problem-solving abilities and excellent English communication skills.
- Self-motivated, goal-oriented, and comfortable working independently in remote teams.
- Experience collaborating or leading teams in dynamic, fast-paced environments.

**Hiring Process:**
- One-hour coding challenge.
- Final interview for shortlisted candidates.

**What's on Offer:**
- Competitive hourly compensation.
- Fully remote with flexible hours (min 20 hrs/week part-time or full-time).
- Collaborative work environment in an innovative AI-focused company.
