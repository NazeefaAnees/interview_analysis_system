
### **Software Developer**

**Role Overview:**  
You'll design, develop, and maintain scalable web and mobile applications, integrating IoT solutions using modern frameworks and cloud technologies (Angular, Node.js, AWS, MongoDB/DynamoDB).

**Responsibilities:**

**Software Development:**
- Develop high-performance applications using Angular, Node.js, and JavaScript.
- Build and manage scalable applications with NoSQL databases (MongoDB, DynamoDB).
- Develop cloud solutions using AWS (Lambda, S3, SQS, SNS, AWS Batch).
- Implement and integrate IoT solutions ensuring real-time processing and scalability.

**Collaboration & Delivery:**
- Work closely with Product, UX/UI teams, and stakeholders to deliver timely features.
- Own end-to-end development, ensuring quality, scalability, and security.

**Problem Solving & Optimization:**
- Troubleshoot, debug, and optimize software for enhanced performance and UX.
- Collaborate across teams to resolve production issues efficiently.

**Continuous Learning & Leadership:**
- Stay current on industry trends, technologies, and best practices (AWS, IoT).
- Mentor junior team members and actively participate in team knowledge-sharing.
- Take ownership, lead by example, and drive best practices within the team.

**Required Skills:**
- Expertise in Angular, Node.js, JavaScript.
- Experience with AWS services and NoSQL databases.
- Strong problem-solving, debugging, and optimization skills.
- Ability to lead, collaborate, and communicate effectively across teams.
