
### **Data Scientist**

**Responsibilities:**
- Collaborate with business units to identify and develop data-driven opportunities.
- Perform advanced data analytics, visualization, and concept development.
- Handle ad-hoc reporting needs beyond data analyst scope.
- Design data integrations (internal/external) in big data or data warehouse environments.
- Work cross-functionally to build and deploy data-driven use cases.
- Monitor performance, optimize models, and refine processes with stakeholders.
- Research industry trends and best practices.
- Mentor junior data analysts/scientists.

**Qualifications:**
- Degree in Engineering, IT, Computer Science, or Statistics.
- 2-3 years relevant experience in data science/analytics.
- Strong knowledge of machine learning techniques and algorithms.
- Proficient in Python or R.
- Skilled in data visualization and clear communication of insights.
- Understanding of data engineering concepts across diverse environments.
