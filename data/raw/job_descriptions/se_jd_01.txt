
**Software Engineer - Job Description**

**About WorkWave:**  
WorkWave provides SaaS solutions for field service management, serving industries like HVAC, Plumbing & Electrical, Cleaning, Lawn & Landscape, Home Delivery, Logistics & Distribution. We empower businesses to deliver exceptional customer experiences and drive growth using our intuitive platform.

**Role Overview:**  
We’re seeking innovative Software Engineers to join our collaborative team of developers, product managers, and UI/UX designers. You'll contribute to building and enhancing WorkWave’s suite of products.

**Responsibilities:**  
- Design, develop, and maintain software applications, including new features and system improvements.
- Maintain and enhance Windows WinForms applications.
- Support development and enhancement of web and mobile applications.
- Write and execute unit tests to ensure software quality.
- Provide technical guidance to team members and stakeholders.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to deliver high-quality solutions.
- Create and maintain technical documentation and best practice guides.

**Qualifications:**  
- Bachelor’s degree in Software Engineering, IT, or Computer Science.
- 3-7 years of experience in .NET framework (C#, VB.NET).
- Experience with React or MAUI (mobile) is a plus.
- Familiarity with Azure cloud services (App Services, Service Bus, Function Apps, Azure DevOps).
- Knowledge of Agile development (tools like Jira).
- Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail.
- Excellent communication and teamwork skills.

**Additional Information:**  
We value flexibility, offering a balanced hybrid environment combining remote work and in-office collaboration to foster community and innovation.
