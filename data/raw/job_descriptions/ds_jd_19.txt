

### **Machine Learning Engineer**

Join our small, dynamic team focused on designing, building, and maintaining innovative ML products that power our business. You'll develop mission-critical applications, ML models, and data integrations using large-scale datasets and cutting-edge techniques.

---

**Responsibilities:**
- Design, build, test, optimize, and deploy ML pipelines in production.
- Research and implement ML algorithms (predictive modeling, computer vision, NLP, OCR, etc.).
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams from concept to deployment, ensuring technical excellence and timely delivery.
- Adhere to established engineering practices, coding standards, and quality controls.
- Ensure ML applications meet client requirements and deadlines.

---

**Requirements:**
- 3+ years experience as a Data Scientist or ML Engineer.
- 3+ years coding experience in Python and/or R.
- 2+ years experience with ML/DL applications (predictive modeling, computer vision, NLP, OCR).
- Solid understanding of data science, ML frameworks, data visualization, and model optimization techniques.
