

### **Machine Learning Lead**

As a **Machine Learning Lead**, you'll drive technical vision, team leadership, and successful deployment of AI solutions serving millions of users. You’ll define long-term AI strategies, mentor engineering teams, and lead innovation in scalable machine learning models.

---

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Lead AI and ML strategy, roadmap, and innovation.
- Develop and deploy state-of-the-art deep learning algorithms and large language models (LLMs).
- Design scalable architectures and optimize AI frameworks.
- Collaborate cross-functionally with product, software, sales, and marketing teams.
- Manage ML project lifecycles, resource allocation, timelines, and budgets.
- Mentor and foster a collaborative and innovative AI engineering team.
- Present AI-driven insights to technical and business stakeholders, driving commercial success.

---

**Qualifications:**
- 7-10 years experience building and deploying ML models in large-scale systems.
- Strong hands-on experience with deep learning, particularly training and deploying LLMs.
- Proficient in Python and ML frameworks (PyTorch, Jax, Scikit-learn).
- Proven track record delivering AI products to commercial markets.
- Exceptional communication skills to translate complex technical ideas clearly.
- Experience mentoring engineering teams and managing cross-functional collaboration.

---

**Skills & Competencies:**
- Strategic and innovative leadership in AI/ML.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving abilities.
- Detail-oriented, with strong organizational skills.
- Effective communicator, capable of engaging diverse audiences.
- Experience scaling AI solutions to millions of users.
