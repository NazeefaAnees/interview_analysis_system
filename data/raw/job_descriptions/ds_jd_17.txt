

### **Senior Machine Learning Engineer – OCTAVE**

Join **OCTAVE**, the analytics and digital insights team of **John Keells Holdings PLC (JKH)**, Sri Lanka’s largest listed conglomerate. We leverage one of Sri Lanka’s richest data lakes to drive strategic decisions, build innovative ML solutions, and positively impact millions.

---

### **Role Overview:**
As a **Senior ML Engineer**, you'll develop, deploy, and optimize machine learning models and pipelines. You'll collaborate cross-functionally to solve complex business problems, utilizing state-of-the-art ML and MLOps practices.

---

### **Key Responsibilities:**
- Design, build, and deploy scalable machine learning models and algorithms.
- Optimize and manage ML pipelines for production readiness.
- Implement advanced MLOps best practices (feature stores, CI/CD, monitoring, etc.).
- Continuously evaluate and enhance model performance and reliability.
- Research emerging AI/ML techniques and integrate relevant innovations.
- Collaborate closely with data scientists, engineers, and domain experts.
- Document processes, models, and technical solutions comprehensively.
- Contribute to code reviews, discussions, and knowledge sharing.

---

### **Qualifications & Experience:**

**Technical:**
- Bachelor's/Master’s degree in Computer Science, AI, Machine Learning, Mathematics, or related field.
- Minimum 3 years of hands-on experience developing and deploying ML models.
- Strong proficiency in Python and PySpark (including performance optimization).
- Expertise in ML libraries: TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-learn, Keras.
- Experience deploying ML models in cloud environments (Azure preferred) and containerization (Docker/Kubernetes).
- Passionate about continuous learning and staying current with ML advancements.

**Soft Skills:**
- Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
- Collaborative, team-oriented mindset.
- Strong sense of ownership, accountability, and project delivery.
- Ability to mentor and lead junior team members.
