
### **AI Engineer**

**About Growth-Prof:**  
Growth-Prof is an innovative fintech company utilizing AI and machine learning to revolutionize banking services. We're dedicated to enhancing transaction processing and customer experience through advanced classification models.

---

### **Job Overview:**  
We're seeking a skilled **AI Engineer** to develop and deploy machine learning models focused on classifying bank transactions. Ideal candidates possess strong ML expertise, software engineering skills, and a passion for applying AI to financial solutions.

---

**Key Responsibilities:**

- Design, develop, and deploy ML models for transaction classification.
- Collaborate with data scientists and product managers to define project requirements.
- Preprocess and analyze large datasets for feature extraction and model training.
- Optimize model performance (accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score).
- Utilize ML frameworks such as TensorFlow, PyTorch, or scikit-learn.
- Deploy and scale models using cloud platforms (e.g., Azure).
- Maintain documentation for models, processes, and results.
- Stay informed on the latest AI/ML advancements and fintech trends.

---

**Qualifications:**

- Bachelor’s/Master’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or related fields.
- Proven experience developing classification-focused ML models.
- Strong Python programming skills (NumPy, pandas, scikit-learn).
- Experience with deep learning frameworks (TensorFlow, Keras, PyTorch).
- Expertise in data preprocessing, feature engineering, and evaluation.
- Knowledge of banking or financial fraud detection (preferred).
- Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.

---

**Additional Information:**

- Competitive salary and benefits.
- Opportunity to work on cutting-edge fintech AI projects.
- Collaborative, supportive team environment emphasizing continuous learning.
- Full-time, two-year commitment (Independent Contractor).
