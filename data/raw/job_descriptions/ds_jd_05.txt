

### **Data Scientist**

Join **Paradigm**, a rapidly-growing AI startup focused on delivering innovative, data-driven solutions. As a Data Scientist, you'll collaborate closely with our expert team to build predictive models, analyze large datasets, and deliver actionable insights for US-based clients.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to identify data-driven solutions for complex business problems.
- Develop, implement, and refine advanced machine learning models.
- Analyze and interpret large datasets to generate actionable insights.
- Stay current with the latest developments in data science and machine learning.

**Qualifications:**
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or related fields.
- 3-5 years experience in data science or machine learning.
- Proficient in Python or R programming.
- Familiarity with ML frameworks (TensorFlow, Keras, scikit-learn).
- Strong statistical knowledge (regression, hypothesis testing, distributions).
- Excellent problem-solving, teamwork, and communication skills.

**Preferred:**
- Experience with Kaggle or other data science competitions.
- Familiarity with data visualization tools (Tableau, Power BI).

Join us and help shape the future of AI technology!
