

### **Senior AI Engineer**

Join **Synopsys’ Generative AI Center of Excellence**, a team pioneering innovative generative AI solutions across the company. We're looking for a Senior AI Engineer to design, build, and deploy advanced AI and ML models, collaborating cross-functionally to deliver cutting-edge technology.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Design, develop, and deploy AI/ML models addressing complex business challenges.
- Collaborate closely with product managers, architects, and stakeholders on AI initiatives.
- Conduct data analysis, preprocessing, and feature engineering for ML models.
- Train, validate, and optimize machine learning models for accuracy and performance.
- Stay current with emerging AI/ML trends and integrate improvements into existing systems.
- Document and communicate methods, processes, and model outcomes clearly.

**Qualifications:**
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or related fields.
- 3+ years of experience in machine learning and software engineering.
- Strong programming skills (Python or C++).
- Solid understanding of data structures, algorithms, and design patterns.
- Experience with ML frameworks (TensorFlow, PyTorch, scikit-learn).
- Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
- Ability to work both independently and collaboratively.
