
**Principal Software Engineer**

**Summary:**  
The Principal Software Engineer leads the software development team, ensuring high-quality design, delivery, and adherence to best practices. This hands-on role involves collaboration with business analysts, architects, QA, project managers, and clients to deliver timely and robust solutions.

**Key Responsibilities:**  
- Lead software development projects, ensuring timely and quality delivery.
- Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams to define requirements, solutions, and project timelines.
- Introduce new technologies to enhance solution capabilities.
- Foster a culture focused on accountability, ownership, and continuous improvement.
- Set and enforce development standards, conduct code reviews, and mentor team members.
- Ensure maintainability and scalability of code.
- Strategically manage team resources, skills, and workloads.

**Qualifications & Experience:**  
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or related field.
- 9+ years in commercial software development; 4+ years leading development teams.
- Deep expertise in Java, plus proficiency in another programming language.
- Experience with frontend and backend technologies, scripting (e.g., Python), and frameworks like Spring.
- Background in cloud-native, high-volume transactional systems.
- Familiarity with AWS (preferred), Azure, or GCP.
- Strong knowledge of CI/CD, DevOps practices, SDLC, API design/integration, and test-driven development.
- Experience in modernizing legacy applications and managing technical debt.

**Skills & Abilities:**  
- Strong understanding of Object-Oriented principles and functional programming.
- Proficient in relational/non-relational databases.
- Skilled in infrastructure-as-code and DevOps tools.
- Excellent communication and analytical skills.
