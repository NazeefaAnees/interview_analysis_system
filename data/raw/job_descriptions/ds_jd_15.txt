

### **Data Science Engineer**

We’re seeking a skilled **Data Science Engineer** experienced in Python, AI, data analysis, and pipeline development. You’ll gather, analyze, and model data, build scalable pipelines, and apply machine learning to deliver actionable insights.

---

**Responsibilities:**

- **Data Collection & Preparation:**
  - Gather, clean, and preprocess data from diverse sources.
  - Automate data workflows for improved efficiency and accuracy.

- **Data Analysis & Modeling:**
  - Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) to uncover trends and insights.
  - Build machine learning models aligned with business objectives.
  - Clearly communicate analytical findings to stakeholders.

- **AI & ML Implementation:**
  - Develop/train ML models using Python (TensorFlow, Keras, Scikit-learn).
  - Experiment with NLP, computer vision, supervised/unsupervised learning.
  - Collaborate with cross-functional teams to deploy AI solutions.

- **Pipeline Development:**
  - Build and maintain robust, efficient data pipelines.
  - Optimize data pipelines for scalability and reliability in production.

- **Collaboration & Communication:**
  - Document analyses, models, and processes clearly.
  - Create visualizations, dashboards, and reports for team and stakeholder use.

- **Continuous Improvement:**
  - Keep up-to-date with industry trends and innovations.
  - Recommend improvements to infrastructure, models, and workflows.

---

**Qualifications:**

- 2+ years of experience in data science, ML, pipelines, and Python.
- Proficiency with Python libraries (Pandas, NumPy, TensorFlow, PySpark, SQLAlchemy).
- Experience deploying ML models to production.
- Familiarity with data visualization tools (Tableau, Power BI, Matplotlib).
- Strong SQL/database skills.
- Excellent communication and teamwork abilities.
- Bachelor’s/Master’s in Data Science, AI, CS, Engineering, or related field.

---

**Preferred Skills:**

- Experience with big data tools (Spark, Hadoop).
- Familiarity with workflow automation (Airflow, Luigi).
- Knowledge of deep learning and NLP.
- Understanding of MLOps and model deployment practices.
- Exposure to cloud platforms (AWS, Google Cloud, Azure).
