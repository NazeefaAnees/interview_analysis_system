

### **Software Engineer – Java**

**Location:** Colombo, Sri Lanka

**About Wiley:**  
At Wiley, our mission is to unlock human potential through learning. We value diversity, innovation, and passion—bring your experiences and join our growing team in Sri Lanka.

**Role Overview:**  
We're looking for Java Engineers (3+ years) to develop and support Wiley’s research, education, and corporate learning platforms. You’ll collaborate closely with architects, engineers, and product owners to build complex applications, ensure high-quality standards, and mentor junior engineers.

**Key Responsibilities:**  
- Develop, test, and maintain web applications and APIs using Java, Spring Boot, ReactJS, REST APIs, and microservices.
- Lead design discussions, provide solutions, and mentor junior developers.
- Perform code reviews and ensure code quality.
- Manage production systems, quickly addressing issues.
- Adhere to Agile and DevOps best practices.

**Requirements:**  
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or related field.
- 3+ years’ experience with Java, Spring Boot, ReactJS, and SQL/NoSQL databases (MySQL, MongoDB).
- Familiarity with microservices architecture and AWS cloud technologies.
- Good understanding of caching, front-end technologies, and web architecture.
- Strong knowledge of Agile methodologies and DevOps practices (CI/CD, GitOps).
- Experience using tools like JIRA and writing user stories.
- Strong communication skills and ability to guide junior developers.

**Nice-to-Have:**  
- Experience in quality engineering and automation.
