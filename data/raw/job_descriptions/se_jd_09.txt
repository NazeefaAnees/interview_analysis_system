

### **Data Engineer**

**About EY:**  
EY empowers you with global scale, inclusive culture, and technology, enabling you to build a career as unique as you. Join us to deliver exceptional client experiences and create a better working world.

**The Opportunity:**  
Join EY’s Data and Analytics (EY-DnA) team to help clients optimize data efficiency, quality, and insights. You'll develop robust data analytics solutions, helping clients make informed business decisions.

**Responsibilities:**  
- Build data pipelines; perform data profiling, cleansing, validation, integration, transformation, and migration.
- Collaborate within project teams to deliver high-quality data engineering solutions.
- Communicate effectively with onsite coordinators and stakeholders.
- Provide regular progress updates and complete assigned tasks timely.

**Key Skills & Experience:**  
- 3+ years of experience in data warehousing environments.
- Strong expertise in Snowflake; experience with Azure Synapse is beneficial.
- Proficiency in PySpark/Spark SQL; familiarity with Scala, Java, or Python on Spark.
- Solid understanding of ETL/ELT processes, data warehousing architectures, and analytics tools.
- Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and debugging skills.

**Preferred:**  
- Specialization or domain expertise in a specific sector is advantageous.
