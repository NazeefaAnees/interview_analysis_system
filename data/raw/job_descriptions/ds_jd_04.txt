
### **Data Scientist**

**What You'll Do:**
- Build machine learning, statistical, and deep learning models to solve real-world business problems.
- Design, train, and evaluate models; architect robust ML pipelines.
- Collaborate with ML and data engineers to deploy models into production.
- Analyze structured/unstructured data to drive actionable insights.
- Utilize frameworks like TensorFlow, PyTorch, and Scikit-Learn effectively.
- Explore research areas such as NLP, computer vision, reinforcement learning, generative AI, and optimization.
- Mentor junior team members.

---

**What We Expect:**
- 2+ years of Data Science experience.
- Bachelor’s degree in IT, Computer Science, or related field (Master’s is a plus).
- Strong Python programming skills and model experimentation capabilities.
- Solid experience in ML/DL frameworks (TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-Learn).
- Proficiency in data preprocessing tools (Pandas, PySpark).
- Experience deploying models on cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP).
- Understanding of statistical inference, optimization methods, and core CS algorithms.
- Familiarity with analytics and visualization tools (Power BI, Tableau).
- Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.

**Nice to Have:**
- Research publications in Deep Learning.
- Open-source contributions.

We seek individuals committed to **Excellence**, **Collaboration**, and **Integrity**.
