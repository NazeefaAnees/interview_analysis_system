
### **Data Scientist**

Join **Paradigm**, an innovative AI startup dedicated to developing groundbreaking solutions. We’re seeking a passionate Data Scientist to build predictive models, analyze large datasets, and provide actionable insights to US-based clients.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to identify data-driven solutions.
- Develop and implement advanced machine learning models.
- Analyze complex datasets and create insightful visualizations.
- Stay engaged with the latest advancements in data science and machine learning.

**Qualifications:**
- Bachelor’s or Master's in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or related field.
- 3-5 years of experience in data science or machine learning.
- Proficiency in Python or R programming.
- Experience with ML libraries (TensorFlow, Keras, scikit-learn).
- Solid understanding of statistical techniques and concepts.
- Strong problem-solving abilities and excellent communication skills.

**Preferred Skills:**
- Experience in Kaggle or similar data science competitions.
- Familiarity with data visualization tools (Tableau, Power BI).
